# Problem 10 design updates

- Kept the sticky navigation visible with a subtle translucent background and clearer focus/hover states, making page changes easy while scrolling.
- Standardized card proportions, typography, padding, and product-image treatment. Cards now use the available desktop canvas more efficiently with a four-column catalogue grid while staying responsive.
- Strengthened product presentation with larger, high-quality image crops, restrained contrast/saturation refinement, consistent shadows, and clear preview/detail actions.
- Added a more deliberate spacing rhythm and page-intro treatment so product information is easier to scan without making the site feel crowded.

These changes make the store feel more credible, make product comparison faster, and keep shoppers oriented as they browse toward a purchase.

## Revision: typography and browsing

- The site now uses Manrope as its single typeface; hierarchy comes from weight, scale, spacing, and color rather than mixed fonts. Navigation and product-detail labels—including size and field names—are larger for fast scanning.
- The professional card system now pairs with filter controls and 16-item pagination, using the screen efficiently without presenting the full catalogue at once.

## Revision: warmer campus-store experience

- The navy-and-white visual system now has brighter Yale-blue gradients, a subtle gold campus accent, and a friendlier “Find your campus fit” filter area. These add energy without abandoning the requested palette.
- Product cards lift and zoom on hover, then reveal a clear detail cue. The result is a more inviting shopping experience that still keeps product imagery, prices, and navigation easy to scan.
